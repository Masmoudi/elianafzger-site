Bio

Elia Nafzger is a London-based DJ and producer driven by instinct, intention and a passion for digging. Growing up surrounded by music left a mark — there's a genuine reverence for sound in everything he does, and it shows in his sets.

He's played Sunwaves, Fabric, Unmute, Trotamundo Festival, Bucharest's Guesthouse and NOD, and rooms in Miami, New York and Berlin. No two nights the same, but always in tune with the room and where it needs to go. He's shared lineups with Zip, Foehn & Jerome, Rhadoo, Fumiya Tanaka, Dyed Soundorom, Sonja Moonear, bobby, Lola Haro and many more.

His releases on Pressure Trax, Analogue, OOZ, Playedby and Below Surface carry the same spirit — raw, considered, and honest. In 2026 he co-founded Body2Body, a new label rooted in the same ethos, with Body Moves 001 marking its first step. In the studio or behind the decks, he's always searching, always digging, finding ways to make it all feel alive.
